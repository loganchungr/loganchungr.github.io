About

My name is Logan. I currently live in New York, but I used to live in Nashville, Tennessee. Before that, I lived in Ohio, and before that, I lived for six months in Seoul, South Korea, which is where I was born.

When I was young, I loved baseball, Carl Sagan's Cosmos, and The Beatles. I still very much love all of those things, though these days, my preoccupations tend to center around language, community ethnography, and the City University of New York.

I have been blessed with a decade-plus career as an international touring musician, audio engineer, event DJ/producer, and political organizer. Below, you can see some of my work:

Frindlers

First Edition (2025)
