6:30–10 pmAutumn Starfest! by the Amateur Astronomers Association

Inwood Hill Park, Payson Ave. &, Seaman Ave, New York, NY 10034, USA

https://aaa.org/event/2026-autumn-starfest/

Join us for Autumn Starfest, hosted by the Amateur Astronomers Association (AAA), on Saturday, October 24, 2026, from 6:30 PM to 10:00 PM at Pat’s Lawn at Inwood Hill Park!
This free, family-friendly event invites New Yorkers of all ages to see Saturn and Moon craters through telescopes supplied by AAA members, along with star clusters, double stars, and other objects in the night sky.
The first 150 attendees will receive free gift bags! Autumn Starfest is a NYC tradition you won’t want to miss. Come and celebrate the cosmos with us! 
Location details: Pat’s Lawn at Inwood Hill Park (enter at W 218th St. and Indian Rd.)
• Train: 1 train to 215th Street
• Bus: Bx7, Bx20
Free and open to the public! All ages welcome. Weather permitting. Rain date: October 25, 2026.

Thursday, November 19

6:30–8:30 pmLegacy of Steve Reich: “Drumming”

Trinity Church NYC, 89 Broadway, New York, NY 10006, USA

https://trinitychurchnyc.org/events/legacy-steve-reich-drumming-november-19th-2026-0630pm
by Sandbox Percussion and students
Join us for a powerful performance that celebrates the enduring legacy of one of America’s most influential composers and the artists carrying his legacy forward. Drumming moves fluidly between different families of instruments — from bongos, marimbas, and glockenspiels to whistling and the human voice — and requires an almost telepathic communication between players. This concert honors the mentors, educators, and musical visionaries who are shaping the next generation.

Registration for this free event is highly recommended as seating is limited.
We suggest arriving 45 minutes before start time. Admission is first-come, first-served, with ticket holders receiving priority (although not guaranteed entrance if the venue reaches capacity).
