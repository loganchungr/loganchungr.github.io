# on Denis Johnson’s novella Train Dreams (2002, 2011)

solid 4. Renny told me about this one a handful of times ago, and now it is apparently a major motion picture. most of all I wanted something completely different from The Sluts, and this delivered; I found something immediately cleansing in the prose's snowy sparseness and delicacy.

at some point I picked up a comparison between this and Hemingway, which is not totally unwarranted, but somewhere in the simplicity, Johnson leaves enough space for some truly astonishing episodes of mysticism which befit the American mountain spirit, East or West.

was struck by the seemingly simple-mindedness of our main character as well as the notions of wildness and deferred animalism. a strong entry, maybe one of the strongest yet, in my survey for an emergent proto-"American magical realism."
