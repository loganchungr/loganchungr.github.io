> I guess that at a certain age, what you might say upon meeting a talented person whose work you respect and/or admire is, well first, the two of you will step outside the cacophonous dive bar because it's simply too loud in there to keep your blood pressure at a level necessary for halfway decent conversation, and outside everyone is smoking, but you're nibbling a tiny, pervertedly sweet cookie, and you are still trying to find a way to say, *No thanks, I quit smoking eight years ago* without feeling that you sound self-righteous, and now that you're outside in the refreshing autumn air, and some kind of homeostasis has been achieved, what you talk about is reality television shows and upcoming Black Friday deals on things like toasters. *It's really good for toaster life*, she may say.   Later she puts her number in your phone and it's only the second instance of that first name in your long list of contacts; you tallied them up before leaving for the bar. You posted it on Instagram, a spreadsheet with all the names that appear more than one times, and already you get to make an edit. Then you notice a fortunate and odd mistake in your data gathering — you'd been slightly distracted, because creating the spreadsheet caused you to run late for the party, which you are now leaving. *Two Kindle,* you may say, *is actually two of you.*
