# general strike, general post

Baby strike today. I can’t wait to see it grow.

Any truly twenty-first century strike will have to reckon with technology. Individual attention has been monetized and is now the oil greasing the gears of the Big Tech behemoths who are propping up the K-shaped economy, their precious markets at the top and our precious wages at the bottom. We have been penned into an informational and cultural environment wholly dependent on our captors. But implicit in that capture is the opportunity for us to intentionally and purposefully direct, shape, and weaponize our attention. Bypassing a 2.5% credit card service fee is one thing — threatening the entire advertising model undergirding the Internet is quite another. Baby steps.
