>
>That said, I can see someone not liking the series for a variety of reasons, but I'd advise anyone curious to commit to reading at least the first two before making a final decision. It's stunning what she's able to accomplish in the first volume but each book has morphed into something else as the series has gone on, so elements that you might find frustrating might get mitigated later on.
>
>I'm very very interested to see exactly how Solvej Balle charts out the rest of her allotted three volumes. Tara, the narrator, is an interesting mind, at times very relatable and at times supremely frustrating, but that contrast is to be expected with a setup like this, and with writing that relays another person's experience of life and (fucked up) time so intimately and with such intricacy.
